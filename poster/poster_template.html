A · Question, Cohort & Method

Why This Matters

Small cell lung cancer (SCLC) is the most aggressive thoracic malignancy and responds poorly to immune checkpoint blockade despite a high tumour mutational burden. T-cell dysfunction in the tumour microenvironment is a candidate mediator, but its transcriptional basis is poorly characterised at single-cell resolution.

Gap. Conventional differential expression captures mean shifts and misses non-linear, context-dependent regulatory relationships encoded in each cell's full transcriptomic rank order.

Approach. Geneformer-V2-104M fine-tuned on HTAN SCLC T cells, then bidirectional in silico perturbation, with findings tested in intact tissue by quantitative spatial pathology.

What Is an In Silico Perturbation?

Geneformer reads a cell as an ordered list of its genes, most-expressed first. Deleting or inserting one gene in that list and re-reading the cell asks a counterfactual question: if this gene changed, would the cell look more like a different disease state?

{{IMG_EXPLAINER}}
Fig. 1 — What an in silico perturbation does: rank a cell’s genes, edit one of them, re-read the cell, measure how far it moved.
Nothing is edited in a laboratory here. The edit is made to the model’s input and the result is the model’s predicted change in cell state, averaged over thousands of real cells — a hypothesis generator, not a knockout experiment. A gene counts as a hit only when deleting and overexpressing it move cells in opposite directions, which is what the next panels report.

Fig. 4 — Quantitative marker-score maps across SCLC tumour sections. T-cell abundance and T-cell dysfunction are scored from disjoint gene panels at every spatially indexed spot, then correlated within each specimen.
Interpretation. In intact tumour tissue, regions with greater T-cell infiltration also carry higher co-inhibitory programme expression — the same association the single-cell perturbation screen implicates, recovered by an independent cohort and a different assay. Effect size varies substantially between specimens, which is consistent with real differences in tumour microenvironment composition rather than measurement noise.
Marker-score proxy rather than formal deconvolution; no Normal or LUAD sections, so SCLC-specificity of the association is untested; correlational design in archival tissue.

Each tissue section is resolved into thousands of spatially indexed transcriptomes. Two disjoint marker panels are scored per spot — T-cell abundance (CD3D/E/G, CD2, CD5, CD28, TRBC1/2, IL7R, CD8A/B, CD4) and T-cell dysfunction (PDCD1, CTLA4, HAVCR2, LAG3, TIGIT, TOX, LAYN) — so their correlation cannot be a self-correlation.
