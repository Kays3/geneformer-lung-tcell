A · Background & Methods

Background & Motivation

Small cell lung cancer (SCLC) is the most aggressive thoracic malignancy and responds poorly to immune checkpoint blockade despite high tumour mutational burden. T-cell dysfunction in the SCLC tumour microenvironment is a candidate mediator, but its transcriptional basis is poorly characterised at single-cell resolution.

Gap: Conventional differential expression captures mean shifts and misses non-linear, context-dependent regulatory relationships encoded in each cell's full transcriptomic rank order.

Approach: Geneformer-V2-104M fine-tuned on HTAN SCLC T cells, followed by bidirectional in silico perturbation screening and orthogonal 10x Visium digital-pathology spatial validation.
