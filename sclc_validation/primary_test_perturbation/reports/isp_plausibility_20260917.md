# Biological plausibility of the complete ISP screen's top candidates

**Assessment only. Plausibility is not validation.** Nothing in this report promotes any
gene to a claim. A gene called "plausible" here has survived a reading of the screen's own
diagnostics and the primary literature; it has not been shown to do anything in a T cell.
No in-silico perturbation (ISP) run was repeated and no existing table, result, figure,
poster or talk was modified.

Generated by [`../scripts/isp_plausibility_annotate.py`](../scripts/isp_plausibility_annotate.py)
against `../tables/allgene_delete_overexpress_shift.csv` (82,702 gene × comparison rows).
Output table: [`../tables/isp_plausibility_top_candidates.csv`](../tables/isp_plausibility_top_candidates.csv).
**Every number in this report is printed by that script.** Re-run it to check any of them.

**Measurement vs judgment.** Every column of the output table is computed from a committed
table, with one exception: `lineage_call` is my literature-based assignment of the canonical
cell type for each gene. It lives in the script as an explicit dictionary, not in prose, so
the counts drawn from it are reproducible and a reader can disagree with one gene by editing
one line. Verdict words — *plausible*, *implausible-likely-artifact*, *uncertain* — are
judgment throughout and are marked as such.

## 1. Selection rule, stated before any result

| | |
|---|---|
| **Universe** | every gene × comparison row of `allgene_delete_overexpress_shift.csv` |
| **Gate** | `delete_fdr < 0.05` **and** `overexpress_fdr < 0.05` **and** `delete_n ≥ 25` |
| **Rank** | within each comparison × direction stratum, by `|delete_shift|` descending |
| **Top-N** | N = 10 per stratum → 6 comparisons × 2 directions × 10 = **120 rows** |
| **Always included** | all 12 rows for S100A8 and S100A9, gate or no gate (`selected_by` distinguishes them) |

Two choices in that rule carry the argument, so they are stated rather than buried.

**The gate drops this repo's sign requirement, and the analysis keeps it.** This repo defines
a concordant hit as delete and overexpress shifts that are significant, adequately detected,
and **opposite in sign** — overexpression pushing one way, deletion undoing it
(`scripts/build_delete_overexpress_shift_report.py`). A **same-sign** pair means the model
moved the same way whether the gene was added or removed: a response to perturbing the token
at all, not to its dose. That is diagnostic, so same-sign rows are kept in the table and
counted (`same_sign_delete_overexpress`) rather than filtered out where nobody would see them.

**Ranking is by effect size, not by p-value.** `delete_fdr` scales with `delete_n`, and
detection count is the confound under assessment — ranking by FDR would rank by the confound.
The FDR-based rank is carried alongside (`rank_gated_by_fdr`) so the divergence is visible
instead of argued. Both ranks are computed over the **gated** rows of a stratum, the same set
selection draws from, with `n_gated_in_stratum` as the denominator.

**Judgment.** This dataset is T cells only (46,140 cells, `ambient_risk_manifest.json`). A
screen over T cells whose strongest movers are surfactant, club-cell, alveolar-macrophage,
fibroblast and neuroendocrine-tumour genes — with **not one** of the 36 canonical T-cell
anchors among them — is not primarily reporting T-cell biology. It is reporting which
transcripts carry disease-state identity in the ambient pool. Geneformer represents a cell
as a rank-ordered list of its expressed genes (Theodoris et al. 2023), so a transcript that
is present at a state-specific rate for *any* reason, including contamination, is available
to the model as a state label. Deleting or overexpressing that token moves the state score.
That is the model using a label, not a T cell using a gene.

This is consistent with the repo's existing conclusion in
`scripts/build_delete_overexpress_shift_report.py` — *"a model-level prioritization signal,
not biological validation"* — and with T4, where the headline programme shift fell **inside**
its expression-matched null (`immune_axis_test/RESULTS_T4.md`).

**CHGA, INSM1, GRP, UCHL1, INA, CALB2, BEX1, TAGLN3, CD24, MDK, TUBB2B.** 21 rows, 11 genes,

rather than *implausible* on one point only: these genes carry the **largest** shifts in the
screen while being among the **least** detected, which is the opposite of the n-driven

produces it. It should not be read as evidence of T-cell expression.

## 4. S100A8 and S100A9

**Verdict: implausible as T-cell biology — likely ambient myeloid contamination acting as a
disease-state label. Confidence: high for S100A8/A9 specifically; the residual uncertainty is
stated below and is not negligible.**

**Four measurements, in order of how much they carry.**

**(a) Neither gene is concordant in any of the six comparisons, and the reason is the sign.**
`concordant = False` in all 12 rows. In 9 of 12, deleting the gene and overexpressing it push
the state score the **same** way. In `luad_to_sclc`, deleting S100A8 moves cells toward SCLC
by +0.0255 and overexpressing it *also* moves them toward SCLC by +0.0113. A dose-responsive
driver cannot do that. This is the single most informative number in the section, and it is
the repo's own criterion, not one I introduced.

**(b) The tiny FDR is a detection-count effect, and the ranks prove it.** In `luad_to_sclc`
S100A8 ranks **12th of 784** gated rows by FDR but **32nd by effect size**; S100A9 ranks 16th
versus 45th. The direction of that gap flips where n is small: in `sclc_to_luad` (n = 31)
S100A9 ranks **3rd by effect but 62nd by FDR**. The 1e-224 comes from 713 detections
supporting a shift of +0.026 — that is a precisely estimated small number, not a large one.

**(c) The detection rate is an ambient rate.** S100A8 is detected in **4.3 %** of these T
cells and S100A9 in **6.4 %** (`mean_expr` 0.057 and 0.088). The T-cell anchors sit at
31–74 %. Young and Behjati report ambient contamination around 2 % typical and higher in
complex tissue; 4–6 % in a dissociated lung tumour is exactly that regime.

**(d) The ambient score agrees — but it is partly circular, and I will not lean on it.**
S100A8 scores `ambient_risk` 0.976 (88th pct) and S100A9 0.958 (81st), both above this repo's
0.950 flag. **However, both genes are `anchors_ambient` in that diagnostic**
(`ambient_risk_manifest.json`) — they helped define the classifier that scores them. Their
high score is therefore substantially by construction and is **not** independent evidence.
The independent evidence is (a), (b), (c) and the lineage literature below.

### 4.2 What the literature says

**The myeloid restriction is primary and quantitative.** Edgeworth et al. purified p8 and p14
— now S100A8 and S100A9 — and found they "comprise approximately 45 % of cytosolic protein in
neutrophils and approximately 40-fold less in monocytes"; the paper's title names them a
protein complex **of myeloid cells** (Edgeworth et al. 1991, *J Biol Chem* 266:7706–13,
PMID 2019594). A transcript that abundant in a contaminating cell type is close to the
worst case for ambient leakage.

**The contrary evidence exists, is real, and is weaker than it looks.** Lee et al. report
S100A8/A9 expression in B cells, T cells and NK cells in severe COVID-19 by scRNA-seq of
GSE158055 (Lee et al. 2022, *Genes & Genomics* 45:337–346, doi 10.1007/s13258-022-01285-2).
That is the strongest published case for genuine lymphoid expression and it is why my verdict
is not "certain". But on checking the paper, **the authors do not address ambient RNA
contamination or myeloid doublets** as alternative explanations. It is the same assay, in the
same kind of dissociated inflamed tissue, with the confound we are diagnosing left
uncontrolled — so it cannot adjudicate the question against us. **Not zero evidence; not
independent evidence either.**

### 4.3 The family is not homogeneous, and the regex is broader than the biology

Pam's T1 filter excludes on `^(?:HBA|HBB$|...|S100A|...)`. In her 50-gene panel the only
S100A members present were S100A2, S100A7, S100A8 and S100A9, so nothing was over-excluded
in practice. **Applied to this screen it would over-exclude**, and the screen's own ambient
scores show it:

S100A4 and S100A6 are detected in ~80 % of these T cells and score as cleanly non-ambient as
the T-cell anchors. **A family-level regex is a lineage claim about a family that does not
share a lineage.** Recommendation, for the axis work and not acted on here: exclude by the
measured per-gene `ambient_risk` flag, which is gene-level, and keep the regex only as a
cross-check.

### 4.4 Where the verdict could be wrong

Stated plainly, because the cost of being wrong here is a discarded true positive:

1. **The ambient profile is unidentifiable in this dataset.** `RESULTS_ambient_risk.md` says
   so directly — the matrix is filtered (min 113 UMI/cell) and already subset to T cells, so
   CellBender could not run and no empty droplets exist. `ambient_risk` is a *behavioural*
   score, not a correction. Nothing here measures contamination; it measures resemblance to
   genes known to be contaminants.
2. **A genuine S100A8/A9-high T-cell subset would look exactly like this** — a minority of
   cells, low mean expression, myeloid-resembling profile. Detection frequency cannot separate
   "4 % contamination in all cells" from "genuine expression in 4 % of cells." Distinguishing
   them needs protein (flow/CyTOF) or unspliced/spliced ratios, neither of which is available.
3. **Point (a) is the load-bearing one and it is dataset-internal.** Same-sign delete and
   overexpress is an argument about the *model's* behaviour, and it would hold even if the
   expression were genuine. It says the ISP result is not a dose-responsive driver signal. It
   does **not** say T cells never express S100A8/A9.

**So the precise verdict is:** the S100A8/A9 ISP result is implausible as evidence of a
T-cell-intrinsic driver, at high confidence. Whether these T cells express S100A8/A9 at all is
a separate and still-open question that this screen cannot answer.

## 5. Limitations

- **Plausibility is not validation, and neither is implausibility refutation.** No gene here
  is promoted or demoted as a claim; this is triage for where validation effort should go.
- The ambient score is a diagnostic, not a correction, and 11 of the top-120 rows are for
  genes **below its 0.5 %-detection floor** (`ambient_scored = False`, including INA and
  CALB2). Absent score ≠ clean score; they are unscored and are treated as such.
- `lineage_call` is my judgment. Counts derived from it are only as good as the assignment.
- Only `delete_*` and `overexpress_*` from one table were used. The goal-vs-alt table,
  per-donor shifts and donor robustness were not brought in; donor-level consistency remains
  an unexamined axis and is the obvious next filter for NBEAL1.
- No ISP run was repeated. If a number here looks wrong, it is flagged, not recomputed.
