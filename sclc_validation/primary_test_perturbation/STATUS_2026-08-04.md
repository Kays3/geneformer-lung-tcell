## Interpretation boundary

Geneformer deletion removes a token from the rank-value encoding and
overexpression moves a token to the front. These are model-input interventions,
not experimental CRISPR perturbations. The final analysis will therefore
prioritize candidate regulators and model-supported state transitions without
claiming biological causality or treatment efficacy.

## Reproducibility and performance

The validated DGX Spark settings are `forward_batch_size=16` and `nproc=4`.
The local benchmark found no material speed benefit from changing either
parameter under this GPU memory/execution configuration. Future optimization
should focus on shard scheduling across additional GPUs, local storage, and
staged screening rather than changing the primary numerical settings.
