Slide 1 · framing

What was tested after the open question?

The original claim read Normal < SCLC < LUAD on a checkpoint axis. We tested whether that one-dimensional reading is supported by centroid geometry and program-level overexpression.

Speaker note: This update is an evidence review, not a replacement of the approved talk.

Slide 5 · interpretation

What we can and cannot say

Supported

  • State centroids are not collinear.
  • Exhaustion co-overexpression moves SCLC toward LUAD in this score.
  • That direction is donor- and stratum-consistent in the completed run.

Not supported yet

  • A curated exhaustion-specific effect above matched null.
  • A strict scalar dose response.
  • An unconditional replacement of the original public axis wording.
