What the new tests actually establish

T3 · geometry

Updated takeaway for review

T3 shows that the states form a triangle rather than a single line. T4 shows a reproducible positive SCLC→LUAD exhaustion direction, but the shift is compatible with an expression-matched random set and the nested dose surrogate is not strictly monotone. These results do not justify replacing the original poster conclusion with an unconditional program-specific axis claim.

  • Carry donor counts and the single-Normal-donor limitation with every Normal-source result.
  • Prioritize donor-balanced/pseudobulk and composition-controlled follow-up before any public wording change.
